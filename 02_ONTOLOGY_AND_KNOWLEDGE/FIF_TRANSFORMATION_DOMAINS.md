# FiF — 10 Transformation Domains (research draft)

**Status:** DRAFT — not canon. The crosswalk to the 7 foundations is a proposal, **à valider par Yannick**.
**Provenance:** "Futures in Focus" (FiF) prototype spec, January 2026 (`FUTURES_SPEC.md`, section "The 10 Transformation Domains"). Content only; the prototype app is not imported.
**Machine-readable:** [FIF_TRANSFORMATION_DOMAINS.json](FIF_TRANSFORMATION_DOMAINS.json)

FiF groups ten civilization-level transformations into six "revolutions". Each domain answers one question ("It changes WHO thinks / WHAT limits us …") and lists implications on three levels: economy, society, individual.
