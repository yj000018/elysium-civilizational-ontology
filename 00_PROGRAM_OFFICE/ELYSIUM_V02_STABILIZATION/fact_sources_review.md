# External fact sources preserved for ELYSIUM v0.2 review

## IPBES / United Nations

- URL: https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/blog/2019/05/nature-decline-unprecedented-report/
- The UN summary of the 2019 IPBES Global Assessment states that around 1 million animal and plant species are threatened with extinction, many within decades. It also reports that the assessment draws on about 15,000 scientific and government sources and identifies land/sea-use change, direct exploitation, climate change, pollution and invasive alien species as major direct drivers.
- URL: https://files.ipbes.net/ipbes-web-prod-public-files/downloads/spm_unedited_advance_for_posting_htn.pdf
- The IPBES advance summary states that around 1 million species face extinction, that 75% of land is significantly altered, and that average abundance of native species in most major terrestrial biomes has fallen by at least 20%; these are dated 2019 assessment findings and must be cited with their assessment context.

## Transport / CO2

- URL: https://unece.org/transport/climate-change-and-sustainable-transport
- UNECE states that transport accounts for about 23% of total man-made CO2 emissions worldwide and about 30% in developed countries on that page. This is an older institutional page; any manuscript use should preserve the scope and date context rather than present 23% as a timeless current value.

## WWF Living Planet Index

- URL: https://www.worldwildlife.org/news/press-releases/68-average-decline-in-species-population-sizes-since-1970-says-new-wwf-report/
- WWF reported in 2020 an average 68% decline in monitored population sizes of mammals, fish, birds, reptiles and amphibians between 1970 and 2016. This is an index of monitored population sizes, not a statement that 68% of species went extinct.
- URL: https://www.worldwildlife.org/news/press-releases/catastrophic-73-decline-in-the-average-size-of-global-wildlife-populations-in-just-50-years-reveals-a-system-in-peril/
- WWF reported in 2024 an average 73% decline in monitored vertebrate wildlife populations between 1970 and 2020. The 68% inherited claim is therefore date/version-specific and should not be updated silently.

## Ecosystem restoration

- URL: https://www.decadeonrestoration.org/publications/principles-ecosystem-restoration-guide-united-nations-decade-2021-2030
- The UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration defines restoration as halting and reversing degradation, resulting in improved ecosystem services and recovered biodiversity, across a continuum of practices depending on local conditions and societal choice. The page describes ten principles applicable across sectors, biomes and regions.

## Review implication

The three v0.2 files contain mostly conceptual completion prose. Most numerical claims are inherited from the parent fragments. They are not treated as newly generated facts. The inherited claims above have authoritative supporting sources, but the final publication package must add citations with scope/date qualifiers. Other factual assertions about technologies, policies, health, economics, or regional impacts require separate verification before publication.
