## 2. Foundation 02 — Vitality: Core Concept

**Canonical name (FR):** Vitalité  
**Canonical name (EN):** Vitality  
**Organ role:** The second civilizational organ — the living substrate that animates material existence.

**Core thesis:**  
Vitality is not a sector. It is the living condition of civilization — the biological and ecological substrate through which all human activity becomes possible or impossible. When living systems degrade, civilizational capacity degrades. Regeneration is not an environmental option; it is a civilizational imperative.

**Core pathology:**  
Industrial civilization treats living systems as resources — extractable, substitutable, externalized. The result is a systematic degradation of the biological substrate: chronic disease, food system fragility, biodiversity collapse, ecosystem tipping points.

**Regenerative direction:**  
Living systems as partners, not resources. Civilization as a net positive force for biological vitality. Health as commons. Food as metabolic contract. Biodiversity as evolutionary capital. Ecosystems as living architecture. Regeneration as civilizational practice.
