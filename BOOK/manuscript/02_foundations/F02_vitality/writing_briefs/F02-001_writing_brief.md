# F02-001 — Health: The Living Condition

## Purpose
Establish health not as a medical sector but as the primary condition of civilizational vitality — the state in which living systems can function, adapt, and evolve.

## Must include
1. Health as civilizational condition, not medical industry.
2. The three scales: individual biological health, community health systems, civilizational health infrastructure.
3. The industrial pathology: commodification of health, disease-treatment vs. health-creation.
4. Chronic disease as civilizational symptom — not individual failure.
5. The social determinants of health: material conditions (F01), food (F02-002), environment (F02-003/004).
6. The regenerative direction: salutogenic systems, preventive architecture, health as commons.
7. Bridge to Food & Agriculture.

## Hard constraints
- Do not become a healthcare policy paper.
- Do not reduce health to individual lifestyle choices.
- Do not overdevelop the medical-industrial complex critique at the expense of the regenerative vision.

## Key idea
Health is not the absence of disease. It is the capacity of living systems — individual, social, civilizational — to maintain coherence, adapt, and regenerate.

## Suggested closing direction
"But health cannot be separated from what we eat. The next vital condition is older than medicine: the food system."
