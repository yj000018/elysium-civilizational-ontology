# F02-003 — Biodiversity: The Living Library

## Purpose
Establish biodiversity not as an environmental amenity but as the evolutionary capital and resilience infrastructure upon which all living systems — including civilization — depend.

## Must include
1. Biodiversity as evolutionary library: 3.8 billion years of adaptive solutions.
2. The sixth mass extinction: rates, causes, civilizational drivers.
3. Biodiversity as functional infrastructure: pollination, soil formation, water purification, climate regulation.
4. The simplification pathology: monoculture, habitat destruction, invasive species, chemical contamination.
5. The link between biodiversity loss and food system fragility (F02-002).
6. The link between biodiversity and health — zoonotic disease, pharmaceutical discovery, microbiome.
7. The regenerative direction: rewilding, habitat corridors, keystone species restoration.
8. Bridge to Ecosystems.

## Hard constraints
- Do not reduce to species count metrics.
- Do not become a conservation biology paper.
- Do not separate biodiversity from the economic and political systems that drive its loss.

## Key idea
Biodiversity is not a luxury. It is the living library of evolutionary solutions — and its loss is the irreversible deletion of civilizational resilience.

## Suggested closing direction
"Biodiversity does not exist in isolation. It is organized into systems — ecosystems — that are the living architecture of the biosphere."
