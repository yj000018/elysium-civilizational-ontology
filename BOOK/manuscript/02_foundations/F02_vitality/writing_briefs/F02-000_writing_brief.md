# F02-000 — Foundation 02 Overview: Vitality

## Purpose
Open Foundation 02 and establish Vitality as the second civilizational foundation — the living substrate that animates material existence.

## Must include
1. Why civilization requires not just material infrastructure but living systems.
2. The distinction between material substrate (F01) and biological vitality (F02).
3. The five F02 facets: Health, Food & Agriculture, Biodiversity, Ecosystems, Regeneration.
4. The core pathology of industrial civilization's relationship to life: extraction, simplification, toxification.
5. The regenerative direction: living systems as partners, not resources.
6. Bridge to Health as the first facet.

## Hard constraints
- Do not summarize all F02 modules in detail.
- Do not overdevelop Agency (F03).
- Do not become a generic ecology or public health introduction.
- Do not list the full 38 Facets.
- Keep continuity from F01 but move clearly into biological and ecological ontology.
- Do not use the word "sustainability" without critical framing.

## Key idea
Vitality is not a sector. It is the living condition of civilization — the biological and ecological substrate through which all human activity becomes possible or impossible.

## Suggested closing direction
The first question of Vitality is therefore the first question of civilizational health: what keeps the living systems alive?

## Cross-foundation relationships
F02 → F01: Vitality depends on material flows (energy, water, habitat) from F01.
F02 → F03: Biological vitality enables economic agency and productive capacity.
F02 → F04: Health, food systems, and ecosystems shape social cohesion and community resilience.
F02 → F05: Ecosystem health and biodiversity condition governance legitimacy and territorial integrity.
F02 → F06: Biological vitality shapes the cognitive and creative capacity of Vision.
F02 → F07: Health, ecological connection, and regenerative living shape Consciousness and meaning.
